Sources for the ouroboros logos (ouroboros.681ed25c). Never shown on screen.

The dragon is drawn by code from geometry, at the owner's request for a set like the impossible logos
(logos.4bc2a2d5): the head is signed distance fields (round cones, ellipsoids and curved horns) meshed with surface
nets (head.681ed25c.js), the body a tube that tapers round a circle into its own jaws, with thorn spines along its
back (dragon.681ed25c.js). The ouroboros, a serpent or dragon eating its own tail, is an ancient public motif; no
image of one was copied.

Material, light, camera and colours are the impossible logos' engine (logos.4bc2a2d5/engine.4bc2a2d5.js): #F0A156,
sampled from the owner's swatch, and the same five rainbow modes.
