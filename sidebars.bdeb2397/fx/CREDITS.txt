(https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji/blob/main/LICENSE). The rest of the sidebar's 3D icons are the creative's
own ../img/fx files.
fx/pack/*.png (the fx pack, ../fx-pack.d3748edf.js: 864 icons in 48 niches, browsed at ../pack.d3748edf.html): Fluent
Emoji in their 3D style, fetched 2026-10-22 by ../tools/fx-pack.d3748edf.mjs from microsoft/fluentui-emoji at commit
1ffb34c752ecf5d402f04cfb4b392c77f57c54bc (assets/<Name>/3D/<name>_3d.png, or a person's assets/<Name>/Default/3D/

file byte for byte; keycap_5 and ringed_planet, whose sources are not square, are centred on a transparent 96px square.

../debug/fx-pack.d3748edf/run.json. License: MIT, Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation, as above.
fx/coin-silver.0ecb8f7c.png (the credits row's coin over the account row): generated 2026-10-21 by
../tools/coin-fx.0ecb8f7c.mjs with fal-ai/nano-banana-pro/edit, a plain silver coin with no emblem drawn in the style
of four of the creative's Fluent icons and the silver of fx/tier-low.8e7c682e.png, then trimmed, its contrast graded
(a sigmoidal curve), cut out with a round mask and scaled to 96px. Prompt and fal request id: fx/prompts.0ecb8f7c.json.
fx/coin-rainbow.0ecb8f7c.png: generated 2026-10-22 by the same tool and model from the silver coin,
fx/tier-max.8e7c682e.png and the same four Fluent icons, cut the same way; not drawn (Billing's 100,000,000 pack is the
silver coin with a CSS rainbow over it, ../settings.854d0793.css .st-flow). fx/coin-gold.0ecb8f7c.png
(Billing's 20,000,000 pack): fal's content checker refused every gold draft, so it is the silver coin colour-graded
through a gradient map (`grade gold`, 2026-10-22). Both logged in fx/prompts.0ecb8f7c.json.

Hotels), Vivid Seats, Uber, Fresha and craigslist, shown as the apps the made-up chats are done in.
