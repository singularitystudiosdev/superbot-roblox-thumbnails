Sources for the pages the chats' computer views show (../screens.bdeb2397.js, drawn by ../screenview.bdeb2397.js). Never
shown on screen.

Every page is a capture of the app's real UI, taken 2026-10-21 at 1470x837 CSS px (Chrome's page area on the creative's
desktop) at 2x, saved as WebP. Each was staged in the page before the capture with the chat's own made-up content
(../transcripts.bdeb2397.js): the names of people, shops, channels, products and hotels are made up, and figures are
illustrative. Nothing was sent, bought, booked, saved or changed on any account or site.

Signed-in apps, captured read-only from the owner's own Chrome (browser-control) with the account's name, address,
photo and counts replaced by a made-up identity (Jordan Lee, jordan@quillnote.app) and checked to leave 0 traces of
the real one before each capture was kept:
  sponsors.webp       Gmail inbox (mail.google.com), staged by ../tools/stage-gmail.bdeb2397.js: the replies to the
                      sponsorship outreach.
  inbox.webp          Gmail inbox, same tool: the 11 starred emails left after the sort, none unread.
  deposit.webp        Gmail search results, same tool: the security deposit thread, transfer and certified mail.
  subscriptions.webp  Gmail search results, same tool: four cancellation confirmations, the gym charge, two statements.
  youtube.webp        YouTube Studio, Content > Videos (studio.youtube.com), staged by ../tools/stage-studio.bdeb2397.js
                      as the made-up channel Weeknight Kitchen; its thumbnails and avatar are ../chats/yt-*.webp.
  haircut.webp        Google Calendar, week of Nov 15 2026 (calendar.google.com), staged by
                      ../tools/stage-calendar.bdeb2397.js; no event was created.

Public sites, captured in headless Chromium (Playwright), no account:

  bike.webp           Craigslist SF Bay Area bicycles, "road bike 56cm" under $600, list view (no listing photos), by

  tickets.webp        Vivid Seats, a real arena concert's event page, by ../tools/capture-tickets.bdeb2397.mjs: restaged

                      facades, no people) and the map is the real one of central Lisbon.
